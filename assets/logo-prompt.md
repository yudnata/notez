# Logo Notvas

File: `logo-notvas.png`

Dibuat dengan tool ImageGen bawaan. Konsep: monogram N putih di latar hitam dengan potongan diagonal yang menyerupai lipatan kertas. Logo dibuat sebagai aset untuk Notvas.

## Prompt final

Use case: logo-brand. Create one finished square desktop app logo for Notvas, a personal notes, checklist and kanban application. Background: solid near-black #141414 extending to every edge of the square. Subject: only a single uppercase white letter N, centered with generous clear space, occupying around 62 percent of the square. Design a distinctive custom monogram rather than a plain typeset N: two confident upright strokes joined by an angular folded-paper diagonal, with a carefully placed narrow negative-space cut that suggests the crease of a notebook page. The mark should still be read immediately and unambiguously as N. Balance crisp geometric construction with subtly softened outside corners. Flat white #F2F2F2 foreground, clean vector-like edges, restrained and memorable, readable as a small Windows app icon. One coherent symbol. No wordmark, no other letters, no page illustration beside the letter, no border, no mockup, no textures, no gradients, no shadows, no 3D, no green, no decorative extra symbols. Output just the logo artwork, not a presentation sheet.
